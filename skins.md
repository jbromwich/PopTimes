# Loader skins — idea list

The Loader's mechanics (conveyor of questions, unit-truthful cargo,
capacity-based levels) are theme-independent. The stats page already
avoids naval language ("level", not "ship") so any skin can plug in.
This file collects future skin ideas.

## Live

- **Cargo dock** (current) — orders ride a conveyor toward the sea;
  correct answers shatter into unit crates that pour into a container
  ship; ship full = level complete; ship sails on completion.

## Ideas

- **Passengers boarding** — the incoming objects are little people
  (person icons, happy faces, or just heads) instead of crates, and
  correct answers board them onto a vehicle: an airplane, ferry, bus,
  train, etc. Same gameplay; the vehicles come in different shapes,
  which naturally varies the "hold" geometry the way ship capacity
  does today. Vehicle full = level complete, and it departs.
